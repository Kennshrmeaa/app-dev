# Harry Potter (Film Series)

## A. Introduction

The *Harry Potter* film series is a fantasy adventure based on the best-selling novels by J.K. Rowling. It follows a young orphan who discovers he is a wizard and begins a new life at a magical school called Hogwarts. Over eight films, the story grows from a lighthearted school adventure into a dark and emotional battle between good and evil. The series is loved for its detailed world, memorable characters, and themes of friendship and courage.

- **Genre:** Fantasy, adventure
- **Release Year:** 2001 (first film); the series ran until 2011
- **Studio:** Warner Bros. Pictures

> A story about how friendship and courage can be stronger than fear.

---

## B. Synopsis

### Overview of the Story

Harry Potter grows up unloved in a cupboard under the stairs at his aunt and uncle's house, unaware that he is famous in the wizarding world. On his eleventh birthday, he learns that he is a wizard and that his parents were killed by the dark wizard Lord Voldemort, who mysteriously failed to kill baby Harry. Harry begins attending Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he makes close friends in Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger. Each year, he faces new mysteries and dangers while Voldemort slowly regains his power. As the series continues, Harry learns that Voldemort split his soul into objects called Horcruxes, which must all be destroyed to defeat him. In the final film, Harry and his friends return to Hogwarts for a last battle, and Harry must decide whether to sacrifice himself to save everyone he loves.

### The Films

1. *Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone* (2001)
2. *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets* (2002)
3. *Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban* (2004)
4. *Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire* (2005)
5. *Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix* (2007)
6. *Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince* (2009)
7. *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1* (2010)
8. *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2* (2011)

## C. Characters

1. **Harry Potter** (Daniel Radcliffe) – The brave "Boy Who Lived" who is destined to face Voldemort.
2. **Hermione Granger** (Emma Watson) – The smartest student of her year, loyal and always prepared.
3. **Ron Weasley** (Rupert Grint) – Harry's funny and loyal best friend from a big wizarding family.
4. **Albus Dumbledore** (Richard Harris, later Michael Gambon) – The wise headmaster of Hogwarts and Harry's mentor.
5. **Severus Snape** (Alan Rickman) – The strict potions teacher whose true loyalties are a mystery for years.
6. **Lord Voldemort** (Ralph Fiennes) – The powerful dark wizard who wants to rule the wizarding world.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Series

I chose *Harry Potter* because it feels like a world I can return to again and again, and it has been part of my life for years.

### Things I Liked

- **The magical world:** Hogwarts, Diagon Alley, and Quidditch make the world feel *real and exciting*.
- **Characters who grow up:** The cast matures with the story, and the tone grows more serious with them.
- **Music and atmosphere:** The theme music instantly brings back the **magic** of the series.

### My Rating

**9 / 10**

### Recommendation

I recommend watching the whole series in order, since the story builds from film to film. It is great for families, fantasy fans, and anyone who wants a story about *friendship and bravery*.
